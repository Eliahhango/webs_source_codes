2-Day Amazing Safari Mikumi NP and Maasai Culture

Kamal Destination Tours

Explore Tours

Experience amazing safari of 2 days from Dar es Salaam to Morogoro region at Mikumi National Park for game drive and village tour for Maasai culture. At Mikumi National Park it is very easy to see a lot animals in a short time because it’s very small covering an area of 3,230 km².

Itinenary

Day 1: Transfer Dar es Salaam to Mikumi National Park & Game Drive
Our driver will come to pick you up at your Hotel in Dar es Salaam at around 5:30 am for transfer to Mikumi National Park that will take about 5 to 6 hours. At Morogoro, you will get hot launch then do a game driver until sunset. See animals such as elephants, lions, etc. After the game drive, you will be taken to Camp Bastian for dinner, sleeping and preparation for the next day schedule.
Day 2: Village Tour (Maasai Culture) & Back Dar es Salaam

Early in the morning before breakfast, you will do a village tour to see and learn the Maasai culture. Then go back to the lodge for breakfast and preparation to leave for Dar es Salaam. The journey will take about 5 to 6 hours. In afternoon, you will have packed launch.
