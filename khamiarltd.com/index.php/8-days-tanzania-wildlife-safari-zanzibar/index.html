Tanzania Wildlife Safari & Zanzibar

8 Days and 7 Night

About the Tour

 The 8 days/ 7 Nights Wildlife Adventure and Zanzibar has designed to meet requirement of visitors with limited budget to spend in northern Tanzania & Zanzibar. You will visit Tarangire, Serengeti National Park & Ngorongoro Crater for 4 days and remaining 4 days for Zanzibar.

Day 1: Tarangire National Park
Depart Arusha at 08:00 am heading to Tarangire national park. It takes only 2 – 21/2 hours (120km) drive to reach the national park entrance. You will have almost 10 minutes for wash room at the entrance while your guide complete legal formalities at the park authority offices before morning & afternoon game drive (Lunch Included). Early evening after game drive you will exit the park heading to the campsite for Dinner & Overnight.
ACCOMMODATION: A.Safari Lake Manyara
Day 2: Serengeti National Park

Today your trip will start early in the morning after hot breakfast. You will depart the Campsite ready for
Spending 2 (two) days at Serengeti National Park. It takes about 4h30 min to 5 hours (250 km) drive up to
the Park entrance. While heading to Serengeti you will transit Ngorongoro Conservation Area and having a
chance to spot some wildlife and see the view of Ngorongoro Crater before reaching Serengeti entrance
gate. (Naabi Gate). After Lunch, afternoon game drive will begin till evening where you will go to your
campsite for dinner & Overnight.</br>
ACCOMMODATION: Signature Camp Serengeti

Day 3: Serengeti National Park

Today the game drive will start during sun rises. After simple breakfast (cup of Coffee/Tea & snacks), you
will start morning game drive around 06:30 am till late morning around 10:00 Am where you will return to
the campsite for a full breakfast. After breakfast you will proceed with game drive till afternoon where you
will exit the park heading to Simba Campsite located at Ngorongoro Conservation Area for dinner &
Overnight.
ACCOMMODATION: Ang`ata Ngorongoro
Camp

Day 4: Ngorongoro Crater

After morning breakfast depart at the campsite with your picnic Lunch descending the amazing Ngorongoro
Crater for spending your day spotting the wonders of Crater. At the crater there is possibility to spot all Big
5 animals (Lions, Leopards, Elephants, Buffalo & Black Rhino). During afternoon you will be served the lunch
followed by afternoon game drive till evening where you will exit the park returning to Arusha for your flight
to Zanzibar.
ACCOMMODATION: Fruits & Spice Hotel

DAY 5: Spice Tour & Stone Town

Spice farm is a trip to the lush green gardens of innumerable species of spices and tropical fruits trees and
learn how spice used in traditional cooking, medicine and cosmetics. Discover strong Zanzibar spice such
as nutmeg, ginger root, cardamom, vanilla, black paper, cinnamon, cloves etc. you will have the chance to
test and touch all spices and visit spice market to buy strong Zanzibar spices by affordable price.
Thereafter you will have the chance to explore the heritage capital city of Zanzibar known as old stone
town. Stone town is navel of historical site, this tour gives guests opportunities to stroll around narrow
street alleys and discovering historical buildings such as House of wonder built in 1883, national museum
(formerly sultan residence) opened 1925, former old slave market. You will also have the time to view
sunset and drive back to hotel.
ACCOMMODATION: Fruits & Spice Hotel

DAY 6 & 7: Relaxing on the Beaches

Full day relaxes at beach and hotel while having a time play at the shore of the ocean
with a very good cold wind and later having the sunset experience from the beach,
your dinner and overnight will be served at the hotel, also you have option for going
out just to enjoy nightlife in Zanzibar.
ACCOMMODATION: Fruits & Spice Hotel

DAY 8: Goodbye Zanzibar – Departure
Today you will be picked up from the Hotel in Zanzibar before transferred to the Airport for your scheduled flight, Here the team of See Endless Adventures will say goodbye and wish you all the best.
